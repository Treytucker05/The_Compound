# Represent Members with customizable Clubhouse Characters

**Status:** Accepted - 2026-08-19

The 2D Clubhouse needs a simple way for Trey and Joe to feel present together. Generic status cards would weaken the retro arcade direction, while realistic avatars or mandatory spatial navigation would add complexity without helping the social or work experience.

## Decision

- Each Member is represented in the Clubhouse by a small, clearly named, customizable two-dimensional arcade character.
- Character customization is visual only and does not grant access, control, or change collaboration permissions.
- Characters communicate presence and social identity; using or moving one is never required to enter a Workstation, share an app, or play a Game Table.
- The first customization set and exact animation treatment remain follow-up design decisions.

## Consequences

- The Clubhouse has an immediately recognizable social presence for both Members.
- Presence remains accessible through names and explicit status rather than relying on visual character recognition alone.
- The system avoids the performance and interaction burden of a full avatar world.
