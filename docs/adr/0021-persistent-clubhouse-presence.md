# Keep Clubhouse Characters visible with minimal activity status

**Status:** Accepted - 2026-08-19

Trey and Joe should feel that the other person is around even when they are working in separate surfaces. That visibility must not turn the Clubhouse into surveillance or reveal a Member's project, application, or AI context.

## Decision

- A connected Member's Clubhouse Character stays visible at that Member's Clubhouse spot while they work elsewhere in the Compound.
- The character displays one truthful, high-level Presence Status, including In Lobby, Working, Watching, Playing, Away, or Offline as applicable.
- Status may indicate Compound activity but must not disclose a project name, file name, application title, prompt, or screen contents by default.
- Connecting, unavailable, and offline states use a clear label in addition to visual styling; color alone never communicates availability.

## Consequences

- Members gain ambient social awareness without leaving the Lobby open.
- Presence has explicit connecting and unavailable states rather than a misleading always-active character.
- The rendered Clubhouse must preserve these labels and provide accessible state announcements when availability changes.
