# Give the 2D Clubhouse a retro arcade direction

**Status:** Accepted - 2026-08-19

Trey wants the Compound to feel like a place to hang out, not an anonymous work portal. The 2D Clubhouse should therefore have a playful retro arcade identity that gives shared games and social presence real character.

## Decision

- The 2D Clubhouse uses a retro arcade visual direction.
- Arcade cues belong primarily to the Lobby, Game Tables, presence, and social feedback rather than every Workstation or Projector surface.
- Work text, code, file names, status, and input controls remain clear and modern enough for long work sessions.
- Decorative lighting, animation, and effects must support a real state or interaction; they are not used as generic visual filler.
- Detailed palette, typography, character design, and motion choices are follow-up design decisions within this direction.

## Consequences

- The Compound gets a memorable social identity without making productive tools harder to use.
- Later visual work can distinguish game and social areas from work areas through purposeful hierarchy rather than repeated generic cards.
- The design must validate contrast, keyboard focus, reduced motion, and readable information states alongside the arcade styling.
